- Audits now keep their findings and turn them into issues: one long-lived row per check and page (or per site for site-level checks), with an open, resolved or ignored state, a regression count, the evidence of the latest audit, and the audit that last checked it.
- Issues are coverage-aware: an audit resolves an issue only when it actually evaluated the page, so a page that was out of scope, blocked by robots.txt, skipped by the page limit or missing from a truncated link graph stays open as "Not re-checked" instead of looking fixed.
- Every cross-page check now runs at finalize: sitemaps, robots blocks, crawl depth and orphans, canonical targets, canonical chains and cycles, broken and redirecting links, duplicate titles, descriptions and content, and hreflang reciprocity.
- Each completed audit carries a health score from 0 to 100 with per-category weights and penalties, the pages evaluated as its denominator, and the categories it did not evaluate, so no data never reads as healthy. Search Console clicks weight the pages that earn the traffic.
- New commands for people and agents: `audits.issues`, `audits.issue`, `audits.findings` and `audits.score` read the list, the evidence, the history and the score; `checks.ignore` and `checks.unignore` accept or reopen an issue with a required reason. An ignore is a settings change, so it passes the policy engine like any other write.
- Finalize is bounded by batch size, not crawl size: the cross-page checks aggregate in SQL over a transaction-local copy of the columns they read, and the lifecycle matches issues in SQL, so a 10,000-page audit finalizes in about 26 seconds with ~18 MB of heap growth instead of holding every page's facts and every link edge in memory.
- Issues now carry their own subject pages (`affected_urls`) separately from capped evidence, so a duplicate-title group does not make each of its pages count as the whole group, and a still-listed but unchecked sitemap URL is not resolved.
- Page findings are keyed by the snapshot that produced them, so two requests that end at the same final URL keep their own findings and a changed redirect destination does not leave stale evidence behind.
- The health score counts only the issues an audit actually judged; open issues whose pages it never evaluated are reported as `uncheckedIssues` and excluded, and site-level issues are documented as covering the whole evaluated site.
