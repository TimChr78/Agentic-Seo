- Add the `providers` package: typed ports for keyword, SERP, domain, backlink, local, answer-engine, Search Console, Analytics, page-experience, index-submission and webmaster data, each with zod-validated normalised shapes.
- Add the provider gateway, the only path to paid or rate-limited APIs: cost estimates, atomic monthly budget holds, settlement to actual cost with 50/80/100% threshold hooks, a tenant-scoped response cache, a Postgres token bucket and a durable `pending → dispatching → submitted → settled | failed | unknown` call lifecycle.
- A caller's authorised ceiling is a control, not a discount: the hold is the larger of the ceiling and the adapter's bounded estimate, and a ceiling below the estimate is refused before dispatch. The ceiling is passed to the adapter, an actual cost above the hold is recorded in full, flagged `overrun` and reported to the `onCallOverrun` alert hook.
- The dispatch intent (`dispatching`, `dispatched_at`) is committed before any call to the provider, so cleanup never mistakes a live call for an abandoned reservation: `dispatching` and `submitted` calls become `unknown` with their hold kept. State transitions are guarded, so a terminal `settled`, `failed` or `released` call is never resurrected and a hold is never released twice.
- Every call carries a persisted idempotency key that reconciliation sends to the adapter, so a lost response is resolvable without a provider task id; a call that still cannot be reconciled keeps its hold and appears in the new `usage.unresolved` read model instead of being silently released.
- A budget's currency is immutable once the period has held or spent anything, and an adapter that reports a different currency than the reservation leaves its call `unknown` with the hold kept, so accounting is never mixed across currencies.
- Add `provider_calls`, `provider_budgets`, `provider_cache` and `provider_rate_buckets` with row-level security, plus the `usage.budgets`, `usage.setBudget`, `usage.spend`, `usage.unresolved` and `usage.estimate` commands.
- Keep raw provider responses for seven days and purge them per workspace through row-level-security-scoped transactions; a deterministic fake adapter exercises costs, ceilings, task ids, failures, currency mismatches and crash-during-submit in tests.
