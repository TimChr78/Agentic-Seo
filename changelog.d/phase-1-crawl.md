- Phase 1 crawl engine: `audits.run`, `audits.status`, `audits.list`, `audits.pages`, `audits.page` and `audits.cancel` crawl a site from a Postgres frontier, extract the `PageFacts` contract with a streaming htmlparser2 pass, and store snapshots, stable pages, link edges and site facts under row-level security.
- Crawl chunk jobs and a once-a-minute reconciler: chunks lease up to 200 URLs, a global Postgres host limiter keeps per-host concurrency, crawl-delay and `Retry-After` shared across worker replicas, and stalled or over-time audits end `partial` or `failed` with a recorded reason.
- Audits record `Coverage`: whether the link graph is complete or truncated by the page limit, depth limit, time limit, robots.txt, errors, a cancellation or a section quota, and the counters and site facts the check catalogue reads.
- A crawl never writes into a finished audit: every page commit is fenced by the audit's state and its own lease, so a worker resumed after a cancel or whose expired lease the reconciler reassigned drops the result instead of adding a snapshot or links.
- Sitemap seeding moved into the worker's first chunk, bounded (200 documents, 1,000 queued sitemaps, 200 MB decompressed, 60 s) and recorded in `SiteFacts`; a command no longer waits on a site's network. Extracted facts keep at most 5,000 links and 1,000 images and say when they were truncated.
- Redirects are followed one hop at a time, checking the site's hosts, robots.txt and the destination host's limit before each request; an off-site or disallowed target is recorded and never fetched.
- A crawl chunk aborts its fetches and limiter waits at its soft deadline or on cancellation, releases the leases it did not finish, and schedules its successor at the end of a 429/503 `Retry-After` backoff.
- `audits.run` enqueues its first chunk in the same transaction when Graphile Worker is installed, so a new audit starts at once; without it the reconciler dispatches the audit, as before.
- Word count and content hash no longer depend on where the HTML parser's text callbacks fall, and microdata on images, forms and links is collected.
- A crawl enqueues its chunks under a job key it derives itself (`crawl_chunk:<audit>:<chunk>`, in Graphile's deduplicating key mode), so an app role can no longer replace another task's queued job; two calls for the same audit chunk dedupe.
- Seeding and chunk cleanup take the audit row lock first — the crawl's one lock order — so page commits, lease release and finalization cannot deadlock; a seed that finishes after its audit was finalized drops its site facts and sitemap URLs, and two seed completions are idempotent.
- A cancellation stops a chunk within seconds even while every request waits on a host, and a successor chunk waits out a backoff another replica imposed instead of resetting it.
- Coverage counts truncated link extraction and truncated sitemap seeding as graph truncation, and manual redirect following stops at the first repeated URL with `redirect_loop` instead of walking to the hop limit.
- Page commits update the audit counters by deltas instead of recounting the audit per page, roughly doubling crawl throughput on the test fixture.
